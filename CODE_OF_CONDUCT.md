# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Our RPG's issues, discussions, pull requests and workshop submissions
to be a place people actually enjoy showing up to — contributors of every
background and experience level, treated with respect. Be someone people
want to build a world with.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being welcoming to newcomers and first-time contributors, including people
  proposing art or ideas through the object workshop who've never opened a
  pull request before.
- Giving and accepting feedback on its merits, gracefully.
- Focusing on what's best for the game and the people playing and building
  it, not on winning an argument.
- Crediting other people's work honestly, and asking before assuming.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions, or reviews.
- Submitting art, dialogue, or code you don't have the right to relicense
  under this project's terms (GPL-3.0 for code, CC BY-SA 4.0 for original
  assets — see [Licensing](README.md#licensing)).

## Scope

This applies to all of the project's spaces — issues, pull requests,
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/dataversion5372/Taiao/discussions),
and object-workshop submissions — and to anyone representing the project
elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/dataversion5372/Taiao/security/advisories/new)
(GitHub keeps these visible only to the maintainer) or by direct-messaging
[@dataversion5372](https://github.com/dataversion5372) on GitHub. Reports
are handled in confidence.

The maintainer will review every report, respond as promptly as they
reasonably can, and decide on a fair response — a warning, a temporary or
permanent ban from the project's spaces, or removal of offending content.
Consequences scale with the severity and pattern of the behaviour, at the
maintainer's judgement, same as any other project decision (see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
